WHAT GUIDES US

Values in action

Our standards shape every decision, from the way we operate our plant to the way we support our people and communities.

Environmental Sustainability

  • Zero liquid discharge (ZLD) implementation across operations.
  • Lowering carbon footprint via circular economy co-products.
  • Maximizing water recycling and conservation technologies.

Safety and Health

  • Zero-harm workplace standard for all plant personnel.
  • Rigorous handling protocols for flammable materials.
  • Continuous safety training and emergency readiness.

Regulatory Compliance

  • Adhering strictly to pollution control board norms.
  • Transparent reporting on environmental clearances.
  • Fulfilling all state and central excise guidelines.

Operational Excellence

  • Driving high energy and fermentation efficiency.
  • Ensuring consistent fuel-grade (99.9%) product quality.
