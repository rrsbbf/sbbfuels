The Ethanol Production Process

How we process agricultural biomass into high-purity anhydrous ethanol

1. Feedstock Selection

Sourcing premium starch and sugar-based agricultural feedstocks like corn, broken rice, and cassava.

2. Fermentation

Yeast is added to converted biomass slurry, cleanly breaking sugars down into ethanol and carbon dioxide.

3. Distillation

The fermented solution goes into complex thermal distillation columns, isolating high-purity raw ethanol output.

4. Dehydration

Remaining moisture fractions are completely eliminated via molecular sieves to yield top-tier anhydrous ethanol ready for petrol blending.

Applications & Uses of Ethanol

Versatile solutions across energy, industry, health, and chemical manufacturing

Automotive Fuel Blending

Acts as an eco-friendly oxygenate in petrol (E10/E20 blends), increasing octane ratings and drastically lowering hydrocarbon and carbon monoxide tailpipe emissions.

Pharmaceuticals & Medical

Serves as a fundamental solvent, antiseptic, and key ingredient in manufacturing sanitizers, tinctures, oral medicaments, and medical sterilizing reagents.

Chemical Manufacturing

Critical feedstock for producing ethyl acetate, acetic acid, ethylene, synthetic rubber, personal care products, cosmetics, and industrial solvents.

Power & Industrial Utilities

Used as clean burning fuel for eco-friendly thermal systems, turbines, power generation units, and industrial heating applications.
